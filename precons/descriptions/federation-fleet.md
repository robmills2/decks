Themes: Card draw, Legendary characters, Spacecraft

## What it wants to do

Federation Fleet is a crew of Star Trek legends backed by a fleet of starships. Start the game by playing artifacts that give you extra mana, then cast Picard and keep the characters coming. Your ships start out as artifacts and turn into flying creatures once you power them up by tapping your other creatures. Most games are won by attacking in the air.

## The commander

Picard draws you a card whenever another artifact or legendary card costing 3 or more mana comes into play on your side. That's almost every character and ship in the deck, so your hand stays full. Captain Janeway, also in the deck, can be the commander instead for a more aggressive game.

## Standout mechanic

Many of the deck's characters have **Seek out**, a bonus for drawing your second card in a turn. Your first comes at the start of your turn, and Picard often provides the second, setting off every Seek out card you have at once. Picard has it too: his grows a creature and powers up a ship. Others make mana or damage every opponent, and [Tom Paris](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/105/tom-paris-risk-taker) gives your whole team flying and double strike (they deal their damage twice) for the turn.
