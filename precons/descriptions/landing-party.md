Themes: Extra lands, Landfall, Free spells

## What it wants to do

Landing Party explores. The plan is to get as many lands into play as fast as you can, with extra land drops, spells that fetch lands, and creatures that dig for them. Every land that arrives sets off the deck's landfall cards, which make Officer and Insect tokens, grow your creatures or power up the Starship Enterprise. Once the lands pile up, huge creatures and free spells take over.

## The commander

Spock beams in a bonus every time he enters or attacks: he reveals the top card of your library and lets you cast it for free if it costs no more than the number of differently named lands you control. A land goes straight onto the battlefield instead, and anything you don't use, you draw. If you'd rather fly, Captain Kirk can take command: he fetches the Starship Enterprise and makes your Spacecraft bigger.

## Standout mechanic

Cards with **Strange new worlds** count how many differently named lands you control: a Forest, an Island and Risa make three, but three Forests make only one. That's why the deck's 40 lands have 35 different names. Besides Spock, [Lt. Arex](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/98/lt-arex-ever-adapting) is as big as your count, and [Lieutenant Uhura](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/46/lieutenant-nyota-uhura) can cast a free instant or sorcery from that many cards off the top of an opponent's library.
