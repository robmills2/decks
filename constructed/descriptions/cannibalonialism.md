Themes: Sacrificing your own creatures, Draining on each death, Bringing back the dead
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

Nobody in this army dies of old age. The deck fills the board with cheap creatures, many of which leave a token when they die, and Bartolomé sacrifices them one by one, growing with every meal. Each death costs your opponents life, and when the board runs dry, the army climbs back out of the graveyard. A well-fed Bartolomé can also knock out a player on its own.

## The commander

[Bartolomé del Presidio](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/301/bartolom%C3%A9-del-presidio) is a 2/1 for two with no table manners. It can sacrifice another creature or an artifact at any time, for free, and gets a +1/+1 counter each time. When an opponent aims removal at one of your creatures, sacrifice it before the spell lands: the spell does nothing, and Bartolomé grows. It dies to almost anything, though, so hold [Dawn's Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/9/dawns-truce) for the spell that matters.

## Opening hand

- Two lands can carry this deck: most of it costs one or two. Bartolomé wants both colors on turn two.
- [Bitterblossom](https://scryfall.com/card/wot/27/bitterblossom), [Bitterbloom Bearer](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/88/bitterbloom-bearer) and [Skrelv's Hive](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/33/skrelvs-hive) each make a 1/1 every upkeep, for 1 life, to feed Bartolomé.
- A drainer plus [Wriggling Grub](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/12/wriggling-grub), which leaves two Worms when it dies, gets the bleeding started.

## Key cards

- [Blood Artist](https://scryfall.com/card/avr/86/blood-artist), [Zulaport Cutthroat](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/126/zulaport-cutthroat), [Cruel Celebrant](https://scryfall.com/card/war/188/cruel-celebrant) and [Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/297/elas-il-kor-sadistic-pilgrim) drain whenever your creatures die, and Blood Artist counts everyone's. [Starscape Cleric](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/116/starscape-cleric) makes each opponent lose 1 whenever you gain life, so the drainers that heal you hit twice.
- [Nested Shambler](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/399/nested-shambler), [Nesting Bot](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/22/nesting-bot) and [Infestation Sage](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/64/infestation-sage) leave a token when they die: two bodies for one card.
- [Raise the Past](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/300/raise-the-past) returns every creature in your graveyard that costs two or less, which is every creature in the deck.
- [Priest of Forgotten Gods](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/83/priest-of-forgotten-gods) sacrifices two creatures: each opponent you choose loses 2 life and sacrifices a creature, and you draw a card.

## How it wins

A point at a time. With Zulaport Cutthroat, Cruel Celebrant and Starscape Cleric out, every creature Bartolomé eats costs each opponent 4 life.

A wipe can finish the job. With Blood Artist out, [Wrath of God](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/279/wrath-of-god) or [Vanquish the Horde](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/49/vanquish-the-horde) takes a life from a player you choose for every creature on the board, theirs included, and Raise the Past brings yours back next turn.

Bartolomé can also win alone: 21 combat damage from one commander knocks a player out. [Angelic Gift](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/19/angelic-gift) and [Feather of Flight](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/13/feather-of-flight) give it flying, and [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/264/rogues-passage) makes it unblockable for a turn.

## What beats it

- Exile instead of death. Cards that exile whatever would go to a graveyard stop every drain, since nothing dies, and leave the recursion nothing to return.
- Losing Bartolomé. [Zahur, Glory's Past](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/229/zahur-glorys-past) and [Dockside Chef](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/93/dockside-chef) can sacrifice in its place, but only once a turn or for mana.
- Opponents who gain life. Each point they gain undoes a point of drain.

## Sideboard

The sideboard's one card never swaps in: [Lurrus of the Dream-Den](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/226/lurrus-of-the-dream-den) is the deck's companion. It starts the game outside the deck, and whenever you could cast a sorcery, you can pay three mana to put it into your hand, then cast it for three more. The price of a companion is a rule for the whole deck: every permanent in it costs two or less. Once Lurrus is out, you can cast one permanent that costs two or less from your graveyard during each of your turns, such as a drainer that died, or another meal for Bartolomé.
