Themes: Legendary creatures, Casting legends for free, Attacking with giants
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

You wouldn't borrow someone's loofa. Make an exception. Every creature here is a legend, and Jodah rewards each of them twice: your legends get bigger for every legend you control, and each legend you cast from your hand brings a cheaper one for free. One card becomes two, and the board gets huge.

## The commander

[Jodah, the Unifier](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1932/jodah-the-unifier) is a 5/5 for one mana of each color, and it plays favorites. Your legendary creatures get +1/+1 for each one you control, Jodah included. When you cast a legendary spell from your hand, exile cards from the top of your library until you hit a cheaper legend (not a land), and you may cast it for free. The free one doesn't set off another.

## Opening hand

- Jodah needs all five colors: each fetch land finds a shock land or triome, and [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/127/arcane-signet) and [Chromatic Lantern](https://scryfall.com/card/drc/53/chromatic-lantern) make any color.
- [Bugenhagen, Wise Elder](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/66/bugenhagen-wise-elder), [Radha, Heir to Keld](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/162/radha-heir-to-keld) and [Ruby, Daring Tracker](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/245/ruby-daring-tracker) are cheap legends that make mana: they bring Jodah out sooner, then grow with it.
- Expensive legends can wait for Jodah, so each one brings a free legend along.

## Key cards

- [Esika, God of the Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/168/esika-god-of-the-tree-the-prismatic-bridge) gives your other legends vigilance and "tap: add one mana of any color", so they can attack and still make mana.
- [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator) draws a card whenever a legendary creature arrives for free. [Garruk's Uprising](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/220/garruks-uprising) and [Temur Ascendancy](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/207/temur-ascendancy) draw for each creature that enters with 4 or more power, most legends with Jodah out.
- [Ghalta, Primal Hunger](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/222/ghalta-primal-hunger) costs less by your creatures' total power, often just two green. Its mana value stays 12, so the free legend it brings can be any in the deck.
- For one mana of each color, [Sisay, Weatherlight Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/29/sisay-weatherlight-captain) puts a legend from your library onto the battlefield. With Jodah out, it can find any but Ghalta.

## How it wins

By sheer size. With five legends out, each gets +5/+5. [Nylea, God of the Hunt](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/166/nylea-god-of-the-hunt) and Garruk's Uprising hand out trample, and when [Odric, Master Tactician](https://scryfall.com/card/m13/23/odric-master-tactician) attacks with three others, you choose how your opponents block. [Aurelia, the Warleader](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/651/aurelia-the-warleader) untaps your creatures for a second combat.

[Betor, Kin to All](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/172/betor-kin-to-all) doesn't need to attack. At your end step, if your creatures' toughness adds up to 40 or more, each opponent loses half their life. Jodah's bonus counts: Jodah, Betor, [Charix, the Raging Isle](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1927/charix-the-raging-isle) and Bugenhagen alone make 48.

## What beats it

- Anything that clears the board: the legends die together, bonuses and all. [Boros Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/148/boros-charm) and [Spectacular Spider-Man](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/14/spectacular-spider-man) can make your creatures indestructible for a turn.
- Jodah is the whole engine. Without it, legends are just legends. [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/501/swiftfoot-boots) and [Snakeskin Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/159/snakeskin-veil) give it hexproof.
- A missing color keeps Jodah waiting, and the seven triomes always enter tapped.
- Cards that let players cast spells only from their hands turn off the free legends.
