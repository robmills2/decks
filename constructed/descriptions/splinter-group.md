Themes: Rats, Aristocrats, Gary from SpongeBob
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

It's a rat race, but this deck wins at a snail's pace. Every Rat that joins your side feeds one Snail, and once it's big, Wick sacrifices it to hit every opponent and refill your hand. The Rats pull their own weight, too: ninjas sneak in to steal and destroy, and two can wipe the board. Put [Phyresis](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/49/phyresis) on Wick, and a 10-power Snail poisons the whole table in one shot.

## The commander

[Wick, the Whorled Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/314/wick-the-whorled-mind) is a sturdy 2/4 that works from the back row. Whenever it or another Rat of yours enters, it makes a 1/1 Snail, or, if you already have one, puts a +1/+1 counter on it. For one blue, one black and one red mana, sacrifice the Snail: Wick deals damage equal to its power to each opponent, and you draw that many cards. Cash it in early for cards, or let it grow into a finisher.

## Opening hand

- Wick costs four, and the blast needs blue, black and red: look for lands that make all three, or [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/298/arcane-signet).
- Rats cast before Wick don't feed a Snail, but cheap ones still help. [Pack Rat](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/73/pack-rat) costs two, and [Voracious Vermin](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/116/voracious-vermin) brings a second Rat.
- A hand of Rats and one land goes back.

## Key cards

- [Lord Skitter, Sewer King](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/97/lord-skitter-sewer-king) makes a Rat each combat on your turn, and [Piper of the Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/392/piper-of-the-swarm) makes one for two mana.
- [Marrow-Gnawer](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/124/marrow-gnawer), [Song of Totentanz](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/150/song-of-totentanz) and [Plague of Vermin](https://scryfall.com/card/pza/7/plague-of-vermin) make a pile of Rats at once.
- The ninjas swap in for an unblocked attacker. [Throat Slitter](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/88/throat-slitter) destroys a nonblack creature, and [Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni](https://scryfall.com/card/v13/13/ink-eyes-servant-of-oni) steals one from the graveyard.
- [Crypt Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/55/crypt-rats) damages every creature and player. Once [Ogre Slumlord](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/186/ogre-slumlord) gives your Rats deathtouch, 1 damage clears the board.
- [Grave Pact](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2615/grave-pact) makes each opponent sacrifice a creature whenever one of yours dies, Snails included.

## How it wins

Usually bit by bit: each blast chips at the whole table and draws the cards to keep going. Marrow-Gnawer and Piper make your Rats hard to block, and [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/313/valley-rotcaller) drains each opponent for every Rat you have when it attacks.

For the big turn, flood the board, then cash in. [Collective Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/132/collective-inferno), naming Rat, doubles Wick's damage, and [Vantress Visions](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/279/virtue-of-knowledge-vantress-visions) copies the blast.

Phyresis on Wick makes the blast give poison counters instead of costing life, and ten poison kills a player.

## What beats it

- Removal on the Snail. With the three mana open, sacrifice it in response and still get the blast.
- Removal on Wick strands the Snail. [Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/311/deflecting-swat) is free while Wick is out, and [Swarmyard](https://scryfall.com/card/eos/42/swarmyard) can regenerate any Rat, Wick included.
- Board wipes, though [Totentanz, Swarm Piper](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/216/totentanz-swarm-piper) and Ogre Slumlord leave Rats behind.
- Your own wipes: the Snail isn't a Rat, so cash it in before [Blasphemous Edict](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/57/blasphemous-edict) or [Swarmyard Massacre](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/20/swarmyard-massacre).
