Themes: Making everyone draw, Punishing draws, Life drain
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

No deck at the table is more generous, and every gift has a price. Everyone gets extra cards, your opponents pay for every one, and your own draws keep you healthy. Point a big Damnable Pact at one player and they're out of the game.

## The commander

[Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse) is a 4/5 with deathtouch that nobody wants to fight, but its real damage is quieter. Whenever you draw a card, you gain 2 life, and whenever an opponent draws one, they lose 2. Sheoldred is the engine, so it draws removal like a magnet. [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/258/swiftfoot-boots) gives it hexproof, and [Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire) can save it from a kill spell.

## Opening hand

- Sheoldred costs four. Three lands and a two-mana rock like [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/drc/52/arcane-signet) get it out on turn three.
- Card draw that's just for you, like [Phyrexian Arena](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/180/phyrexian-arena), is great early. Save the cards that make everyone draw until Sheoldred is out, or you're feeding three opponents for free.
- Mulligan hands that can't reach four mana by turn four.

## Key cards

- [Underworld Dreams](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/121/underworld-dreams), [Fate Unraveler](https://scryfall.com/card/bng/68/fate-unraveler), [Scrawling Crawler](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/132/scrawling-crawler) and [Ob Nixilis, the Hate-Twisted](https://scryfall.com/card/war/100/ob-nixilis-the-hate-twisted) are smaller Sheoldreds: each card an opponent draws costs them 1 more. They keep the pressure on when Sheoldred is gone.
- [Howling Mine](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/300/howling-mine), [Font of Mythos](https://scryfall.com/card/con/136/font-of-mythos) and [Seizan, Perverter of Truth](https://scryfall.com/card/mul/15/seizan-perverter-of-truth) give everyone extra cards every turn, and Sheoldred bills each one.
- Spells that say "target player draws", like [Damnable Pact](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/139/damnable-pact), can refill your hand, but they're best aimed at an opponent. After the first cast, [Decorum Dissertation](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/78/decorum-dissertation) gives you a free copy on each of your turns.
- [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/92/bloodletter-of-aclazotz) doubles all the life your opponents lose on your turn.
- [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/362/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose) and [Sanguine Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/wot/35/sanguine-bond) turn the life you gain into life an opponent loses.

## How it wins

Most games, the table bleeds out a little at a time until [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate), [Torment of Hailfire](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/77/torment-of-hailfire) or [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/89/gray-merchant-of-asphodel) can finish it. [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/106/cabal-coffers) and [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/61/crypt-ghast) make the mana for a big X.

The quick kill is Damnable Pact aimed at one opponent. They draw X cards and lose X life, and Sheoldred takes 2 more for each card: 3X in all. With Bloodletter out it's 6X, so seven mana takes 30 life.

## What beats it

- Killing Sheoldred again and again. It comes back from the command zone, but costs 2 more each time, and the smaller Sheoldreds only do half its job.
- Opponents who put your gifts to work, and win with them before the life loss catches up.
- Artifact and enchantment removal: most of the engines are one or the other.
- Your own life total. Cards like [Greed](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/274/greed) charge you life, and without Sheoldred's lifegain, that adds up.
- Cards that stop players drawing more than one card a turn shut off most of the plan.

## Sideboard

For a Bracket 3 table, take out Price of Knowledge and put in [Peer into the Abyss](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/117/peer-into-the-abyss). It makes a player draw half their library and lose half their life. Aimed at an opponent while Sheoldred is out, that's a kill: they draw a pile of cards at 2 life each. Even without Sheoldred, Bloodletter of Aclazotz makes it a kill, and Underworld Dreams or Ob Nixilis usually do. That's a two-card kill with your commander, which is why it waits for Bracket 3.
