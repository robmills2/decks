Themes: Gaining life in small bites, Breeding Pests and Insects, Growing the whole swarm
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

This deck is the manifestation of a guys' toho bathroom after spring finals. Pests, Insects and Spiders breed in every corner, and Blech makes them all bigger every time you gain life. Gain life in small bites, over and over, and the swarm grows until it tramples the table. If the attack stalls, the same lifegain drains everyone instead.

## The commander

[Blech, Loafing Pest](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/176/blech-loafing-pest) is a 3/4 for three that lets everyone else do the work. Whenever you gain life, it puts a +1/+1 counter on each Pest, Bat, Insect, Snake and Spider you control, itself included. It counts how often you gain life, not how much: five gains of 1 life make five counters on each, and one gain of 5 makes one. Bring dice.

## Opening hand

- Blech needs black and green by turn three. [Farseek](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/50/farseek) and [Nature's Lore](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/101/natures-lore) can each fetch [Overgrown Tomb](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/284/overgrown-tomb), which makes both.
- [Kazandu Nectarpot](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/306/kazandu-nectarpot) and [Eumidian Terrabotanist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/180/eumidian-terrabotanist) gain a life for each land you play, and they're Insects, so Blech grows them too.
- Many small pieces beat one big card here: keep two or three lands and some cheap creatures.

## Key cards

- [Essence Warden](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/145/essence-warden), [Bogwater Lumaret](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/177/bogwater-lumaret) and [Virulent Emissary](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/202/virulent-emissary) gain a life for every creature that joins your side, and Essence Warden counts everyone's. Each is a separate gain, so four new Pests mean four rounds of counters.
- [Hardened Scales](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/133/hardened-scales), [Winding Constrictor](https://scryfall.com/card/aer/140/winding-constrictor) and [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/158/doubling-season) make every round of counters bigger.
- [Pest Infestation](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/282/pest-infestation) and [Tend the Pests](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/242/tend-the-pests) make Pests in bulk, each worth a life when it dies. Tend the Pests trades a creature Blech has fed for a Pest per point of its power.
- [Whip of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/110/whip-of-erebos) hands out lifelink, and every creature that deals damage counts as its own gain.

## How it wins

Mostly by walking over the table. [Duskshell Crawler](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/301/duskshell-crawler) gives trample to every creature with a +1/+1 counter, and [Ohran Frostfang](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/309/ohran-frostfang) gives your attackers deathtouch and draws a card for each one that hits a player. Together, each blocker takes just 1 damage, and the rest goes through.

The drain is the backup. [Enduring Tenacity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/95/enduring-tenacity) and [Sanguine Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/208/sanguine-bond) make an opponent lose as much life as you gain, and [Starscape Cleric](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/116/starscape-cleric) and [South Wind Avatar](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/78/south-wind-avatar) make each opponent lose 1 per gain. [Blood Artist](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/209/blood-artist) and [Cauldron of Essence](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/179/cauldron-of-essence) drain as creatures die, and the life they gain feeds Blech too.

## What beats it

- A single wipe flushes the swarm, counters and all. [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/34/heroic-intervention) and [Inspiring Call](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/226/inspiring-call) keep it standing for a turn, and [The Ozolith](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/362/the-ozolith) hangs on to the counters and hands them to one creature at your next combat.
- Cards that stop lifegain leave Blech with nothing to do but loaf.
- Without Blech, the swarm stops growing, though [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid) still adds counters to everything at each of your combats.
