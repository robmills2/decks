Themes: Elves, Faeries, Stealing cards
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

The cards in this deck are small. The cards in your opponents' decks are not, and this deck helps itself to them. Every Elf or Faerie that enters exiles the top two cards of an opponent's library, and once a turn you can cast one of those cards for free. The more Elves and Faeries you have, the bigger the spell you can steal, so the deck floods the board with cheap ones.

## The commander

[Maralen, Fae Ascendant](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/233/maralen-fae-ascendant) is a 4/5 flyer, sturdy but pricey for a deck of one-drops. She is an Elf and a Faerie at once, so she counts for both, and her own arrival starts the stealing. The spell you cast must have a mana value no bigger than your Elf and Faerie count, and come from cards exiled that turn. Protect her with [Lightning Greaves](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/349/lightning-greaves), [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/who/233/heroic-intervention) or [Snakeskin Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/159/snakeskin-veil).

## Opening hand

- Keep two or three lands and a one-mana creature, such as [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/7167/llanowar-elves), [Elvish Mystic](https://scryfall.com/card/m15/173/elvish-mystic), [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/drk/76/elves-of-deep-shadow) or [Arbor Elf](https://scryfall.com/card/m13/160/arbor-elf). [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2683/sol-ring) beats them all.
- Maralen needs black, green and blue. [Urborg Elf](https://scryfall.com/card/apc/90/urborg-elf) makes all three.
- Any cheap Elf or Faerie is welcome: once Maralen is out, each one steals.
- A hand that can't make green is a mulligan, since nearly every early play needs it.

## Key cards

- [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2415/roaming-throne): name Elf or Faerie, and Maralen's trigger happens twice, so each arrival exiles four cards.
- [Springleaf Parade](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/19/springleaf-parade) makes X changeling tokens, and each one is another trigger. [Elvish Promenade](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/208/elvish-promenade) makes an Elf for every Elf you control.
- [Bitterblossom](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1898/bitterblossom) and [Bitterbloom Bearer](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/310/bitterbloom-bearer) add a Faerie every upkeep, so Maralen steals every turn for a little life.
- [High Fae Trickster](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/40/high-fae-trickster) lets you cast spells as though they had flash. Flash Faeries entering on an opponent's turn can then steal a sorcery and cast it right there.

## How it wins

There's no combo. Maralen takes the best cards off the top of opponents' decks while the board grows, and the board does the rest. [Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://scryfall.com/card/som/119/ezuri-renegade-leader) gives every Elf +3/+3 and trample for five mana, a lethal swing from a pile of small creatures. [Lathril, Blade of the Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2681/lathril-blade-of-the-elves) can tap ten other Elves to make each opponent lose 10 life while you gain 10. [Obyra, Dreaming Duelist](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/210/obyra-dreaming-duelist) drains each opponent whenever another Faerie arrives.

## What beats it

- Creature wipes. Most of the deck is 1/1s and 2/2s. Heroic Intervention saves the board from destroy effects, and [Raise the Palisade](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/106/raise-the-palisade) can send everything but your own Elves back to hand.
- Killing Maralen before the board is wide. Without her, it's a pile of small creatures.
- Early on, stolen spells are often too big to cast, and they stay exiled.
- A stolen spell can still be countered, and the deck has plenty of one-for-one answers but few for a whole board.
