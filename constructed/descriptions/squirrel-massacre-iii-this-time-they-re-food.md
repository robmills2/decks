Themes: Making and eating Food, Building a Squirrel horde, Draining the table
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

The Squirrels don't survive this one either. The deck makes Food tokens, artifacts you can sacrifice for 3 life at two mana a bite, and eats them for something better: each one becomes a Squirrel, and drain cards charge your opponents for every token that comes and goes. The survivors become a horde. With the right two cards out, one Food starts an endless loop.

## The commander

[Camellia, the Seedmiser](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/207/camellia-the-seedmiser) is a 3/3 for three that wastes nothing. It and your other Squirrels have menace, so each takes two blockers. Whenever you sacrifice Foods, it makes a 1/1 Squirrel: one per batch, however many go at once. For two mana and a Food, or three cards exiled from your graveyard, it puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your other Squirrels.

## Opening hand

- [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/227/llanowar-elves) and [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/ddj/47/elves-of-deep-shadow) can bring Camellia out on turn two.
- [Gilded Goose](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/221/gilded-goose) arrives with a Food and turns Food into mana: Camellia's first meal.
- Six spells, like [Fell the Profane](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/244/fell-the-profane-fell-mire), have a land on the back. Count them as lands when judging a hand.

## Key cards

- [Chatterfang, Squirrel General](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/95/chatterfang-squirrel-general) adds a Squirrel for every token you make, and [Tippy-Toe, Terrific Partner](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/734/tippy-toe-terrific-partner) adds a Food to every batch.
- [Ygra, Eater of All](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/241/ygra-eater-of-all) is the hidden second commander. It makes every other creature a Food, so sacrificed Squirrels feed Camellia, and Ygra gets two +1/+1 counters with every death.
- [Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/421/mirkwood-bats), [Marionette Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/100/marionette-apprentice), [Nadier's Nightblade](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/184/nadiers-nightblade) and [Pactdoll Terror](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/99/pactdoll-terror) each take 1 life from every opponent as tokens come and go: with all four, one Food costs each opponent at least 5.
- [Parallel Lives](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/36/parallel-lives) and [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/557/doubling-season) double every token, and [The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl](https://scryfall.com/card/msh/193/the-unbeatable-squirrel-girl) doubles your Squirrels for four mana, again and again.

## How it wins

Usually by nibbling: the drain cards take a life here, a life there, and [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/313/valley-rotcaller) drains each opponent 1 for each other Squirrel you control when it attacks.

Or the horde runs everyone over. [Beastmaster Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/118/beastmaster-ascension) gives your creatures +5/+5 once they've attacked seven times in all, and [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2680/finale-of-devastation) for X of 10 or more gives them +X/+X and haste.

Then there's the loop. With Camellia, [Ninja Pizza](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/32/ninja-pizza) and Tippy-Toe out, tap and sacrifice a Food for one mana. Camellia makes a Squirrel, and Tippy-Toe adds a Food. Repeat for endless mana and Squirrels: any drain card finishes the table. Ygra and [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/slc/16/phyrexian-altar) loop the same way with Squirrels.

## What beats it

- A wipe takes the whole horde at once, though each death still drains with Marionette Apprentice or Nadier's Nightblade out. [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/34/heroic-intervention) and [For the Common Good](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/321/for-the-common-good) can save it.
- Mass artifact removal clears out every Food and Treasure.
- Without Camellia, Foods stop turning into Squirrels. [Lightning Greaves](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/217/lightning-greaves) gives it shroud.
