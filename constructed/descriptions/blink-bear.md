Themes: Blinking your commander, Stacking Shrines, Spirit tokens
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

Now you see it, now you don't. This is a blink deck and a Shrine deck in one. Blink spells exile Hei Bai and bring it straight back, and each time it returns, it brings a Shrine with it. Shrines get stronger the more of them you control: one barely matters, but a pile of them takes over the game.

## The commander

[Hei Bai, Forest Guardian](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/139/hei-bai-forest-guardian) is a 4/4 for four who never arrives alone. When it enters, it digs through your library to the first Shrine and puts that Shrine onto the battlefield. For one mana of each color and a tap, it makes a 1/1 Spirit for each legendary enchantment you control, and every Shrine in the deck is one. Only Spirits can block them. Blink it when a removal spell targets it: the spell misses, and you get another Shrine.

## Opening hand

- [Birds of Paradise](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/170/birds-of-paradise), [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/227/llanowar-elves) and [Delighted Halfling](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/363/delighted-halfling) get Hei Bai out a turn early, on turn three.
- Each fetch land can grab a two- or three-color land, so five colors is easier than it looks.
- A one-mana blink spell like [Cloudshift](https://scryfall.com/card/avr/12/cloudshift) or [Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate) gets a second Shrine soon after it lands.
- Throw back hands with no green source: Hei Bai and the early mana creatures all need it.

## Key cards

- [Flicker](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/9/flicker) and [Flicker of Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/16/flicker-of-fate) can blink a Shrine instead: [Northern Air Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/111/northern-air-temple) drains the table again, and [The Spirit Oasis](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/72/the-spirit-oasis) draws you another handful.
- [Soulherder](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/214/soulherder) blinks a creature at the end of each of your turns. Once you pay six to harness [The Mind Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/msh/386/the-mind-stone), it does the same, and can pick a Shrine.
- [Sanctum of All](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/225/sanctum-of-all) puts a Shrine onto the battlefield every upkeep, and once you control six, the other Shrines' abilities trigger twice.
- [Starfield Vocalist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/78/starfield-vocalist) doubles your abilities that trigger when something enters, so Hei Bai finds two Shrines at a time.

## How it wins

Slowly, then all at once. On each of your turns, [Honden of Infinite Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/134/honden-of-infinite-rage) deals damage equal to the number of Shrines you control, [Sanctum of Stone Fangs](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/120/sanctum-of-stone-fangs) drains each opponent for the same, and [Honden of Life's Web](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/172/honden-of-lifes-web) makes that many Spirits. [Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/card/wot/44/impact-tremors) and [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/14/purphoros-god-of-the-forge) hit every opponent whenever one of your creatures enters, tokens and blinks included. Hei Bai's Spirits finish the job.

## What beats it

- Mass enchantment removal: one spell takes every Shrine, and the cards that tax attackers too.
- Anything that stops abilities from triggering when a creature enters turns Hei Bai into a plain 4/4.
- Speed. Stacking Shrines takes a few turns, and until then [Ghostly Prison](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/116/ghostly-prison), [Propaganda](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/80/propaganda) and [Sphere of Safety](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/24/sphere-of-safety) make attacking you expensive.
- Creature wipes take Hei Bai and the Spirits, but most Shrines stay. [Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1691/teferis-protection) saves the whole board, and [Eerie Interlude](https://scryfall.com/card/ddq/8/eerie-interlude) brings Hei Bai back with another Shrine.
