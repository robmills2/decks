Themes: +1/+1 counters, Opening Attractions, Commander damage
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

Aptronym (noun): look it up, nerd. Attractions are artifacts kept in a deck of their own. Opening one puts the top card onto the battlefield. On your turn, you roll a die, and every Attraction with that number lit up gives you a little something, like a card or a token. Each one opened while TMDG is out gives it a +1/+1 counter, and the deck multiplies them until it can knock out a player with 21 commander damage.

## The commander

[The Most Dangerous Gamer](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/174/the-most-dangerous-gamer) is a 2/2 for four that fights dirty: with deathtouch, any creature it damages dies. It opens an Attraction when it enters or attacks, and gets a +1/+1 counter whenever you open one, its own or not. Claiming an Attraction's prize lets it destroy a permanent, but only [Pick-a-Beeble](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/223a/pick-a-beeble) has a prize here, so that's a bonus.

## Opening hand

- TMDG costs four, with black and green. [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/fdc/2/sol-ring), [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/227/llanowar-elves) or [Rampant Growth](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/313/rampant-growth) speeds it up.
- An early [Hardened Scales](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/133/hardened-scales) or [Winding Constrictor](https://scryfall.com/card/aer/140/winding-constrictor) adds one extra to every batch of counters that follows.
- One land is never enough, however good the rest looks.

## Key cards

- [Corpsejack Menace](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/56/corpsejack-menace) and [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/428/doubling-season) double every batch of counters. With Doubling Season and Hardened Scales, each Attraction gives TMDG four.
- At each of your combats, [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/345/ouroboroid) puts counters equal to its power on each of your creatures, itself included, so the number climbs every turn.
- [Duskshell Crawler](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/156/duskshell-crawler), [Kodama of the West Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/78/kodama-of-the-west-tree) and [Power Fist](https://scryfall.com/card/pip/81/power-fist) give TMDG trample. With deathtouch, one damage kills each blocker, and the rest hits the player.
- [My Precious](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/176/my-precious-allure-of-power) makes it unblockable, and [Canopy Cover](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/98/canopy-cover) nearly so. With either, opponents can't target it.
- Every Attraction has 6 lit up, so a 6 visits them all. [Night Shift of the Living Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/81/night-shift-of-the-living-dead) turns a 5 into a 6 for 1 life.

## How it wins

One player at a time: 21 combat damage from TMDG knocks a player out. It gains a few counters a turn, so the first knockout takes several swings, and each one after comes faster.

When counters pile up, the whole team joins in. [Bristly Bill, Spine Sower](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/157/bristly-bill-spine-sower) doubles the counters on each of your creatures for five mana, and [Swinging Ship](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/231a/swinging-ship), an Attraction, adds a second combat.

## What beats it

- A kill spell on TMDG before it grows. [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/355/swiftfoot-boots) and [Snakeskin Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/233/snakeskin-veil) give it hexproof, and if it dies, [Host of the Hereafter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/193/host-of-the-hereafter) or [The Ozolith](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/362/the-ozolith) passes its counters to another creature.
- Board wipes take every counter with them. Save [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/34/heroic-intervention) or [Inspiring Call](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/226/inspiring-call) for them.
- Combo decks and fast armies. The deck has three removal cards, like [Black Hole](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/67/black-hole) and [Saw in Half](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/88/saw-in-half), and one nine-mana wipe, so it can't slow them down much.
- Chump blockers, until TMDG has trample.
