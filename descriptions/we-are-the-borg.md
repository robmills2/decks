Themes: Artifact creatures, Borg tokens, Stealing creatures

## What it wants to do

Every creature in this deck is a Borg, and the Borg share everything: more than a dozen cards hand an upgrade to all your artifact creatures, so each new drone makes the whole collective stronger. The Queen costs seven mana, so expect a slow start spent playing artifacts. Then the deck floods the board with 1/1 Borg tokens and stolen creatures, and the collective grows until nothing can stand in its way.

## The commander

The Borg Queen gives your artifact creatures +2/+2, and whenever she arrives or attacks, she digs into an opponent's library for its first creature and adds it to your forces. Getting her out is the hard part, so protect her once she lands. If seven mana is too long a wait, Locutus of Borg makes a four-mana commander instead: every artifact creature of yours that hits a player brings another Borg token with it.

## Standout mechanic

**Assimilate** means taking an opponent's creature and making it yours, with a +1/+1 counter. It becomes a Borg artifact creature, so it gets every upgrade the collective shares. [Gift of Perfection](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/44/gift-of-perfection) makes every opponent reveal cards until they hit a creature, then lets you take your pick, and [Assimilation Assessor](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/56/assimilation-assessor) exiles an opponent's graveyard, then brings back one of its creatures for you each time it attacks.
