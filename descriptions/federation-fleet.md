Themes: Card draw, Legendary characters, Spacecraft

## What it wants to do

Federation Fleet turns extra cards into a growing crew and a fleet of starships. Most of its creatures are legendary Star Trek characters, and many have Seek out: a bonus that happens whenever you draw your second card in a turn. Its Spacecraft start out as artifacts and become flying creatures once they collect enough charge counters, which you add by tapping your other creatures. Early turns go to mana-making artifacts. Once Picard arrives, the deck draws steadily, builds its board and usually wins by attacking in the air.

## The commander

Jean-Luc Picard draws a card whenever another artifact or legendary card costing 3 or more mana enters under your control, and more than 30 cards in the deck qualify. That draw is often your second of the turn, so it sets off every Seek out card you have, including Picard's own, which puts a +1/+1 counter on another creature and two charge counters on a Spacecraft. For a more aggressive game, Captain Janeway, also in the deck, can be the commander instead.

## Combos and synergies

- [Jean-Luc Picard](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/3/jean-luc-picard-luminary) and [Tom Paris](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/105/tom-paris-risk-taker). With Picard out, casting Tom draws a card. If that's your second of the turn, Tom's Seek out goes off at once: until the turn ends, all your creatures fly, can attack right away and deal their combat damage twice. It takes six mana with Picard already out, and Tom is one card in 99, so expect it in about one game in six or seven.
- Otherwise the deck has no single winning combination: extra draws set off Seek out, which grows your creatures and charges your ships.
