Themes: All-out attacks, Klingon Warriors, Team boosts

## What it wants to do

Klingon Fury never stops attacking. It gets cheap Warriors onto the table early, then keeps the war band growing with Warrior tokens that attack the moment they arrive, cards that make your whole team hit harder, and a few ways to attack twice in one turn. A few cards even force your opponents' creatures to attack each other, so the whole table ends up at war.

## The commander

Worf arrives with a 2/1 Klingon Warrior that can attack right away. Whenever you attack, he exiles the top card of your library for each opponent you attacked, and you can play those cards until the end of your next turn. Kor, the deck's backup commander, takes a different route: each time you attack, he lets you sacrifice one of your creatures to draw a card and make your Klingons and Warriors bigger.

## Standout mechanic

Worf is one of eight cards with **Battlefront**, which counts how many opponents you attacked this turn, so it pays to spread your attackers across the table instead of picking on one player. In a four-player game, attacking all three opponents means [Kurn](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/60/kurn-son-of-mogh) draws you three cards for 3 life and [Rite of Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/103/rite-of-ascension) adds three more Warriors to the attack.
