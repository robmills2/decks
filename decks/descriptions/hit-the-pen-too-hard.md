Themes: Spreading -1/-1 counters, Stealing creatures, Chain reactions
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

Sometimes dead is better. The deck spreads -1/-1 counters around the table, shrinking creatures until they die, and once a turn the Reaper claims one of the dead for you. Counters can also make tokens or drain life, and with the right three cards out, one counter starts a chain reaction that drains the whole table.

## The commander

[The Reaper, King No More](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/4/the-reaper-king-no-more) is a 3/3 Scarecrow with sticky fingers. Its cost looks like six, but with black, red and green it costs only three. When it enters, it puts a -1/-1 counter on up to two creatures. Once each turn, when an opponent's creature with a -1/-1 counter dies, you may put it onto the battlefield on your side. You keep it even if the Reaper dies.

## Opening hand

- Three lands and a two-drop is a fine start. [Delighted Halfling](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/158/delighted-halfling) makes any color for the Reaper, so it can arrive on turn two.
- Missing a color? Pay two mana of any kind in its place.
- [Dread Tiller](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/20/dread-tiller) and [Spitting Dilophosaurus](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/3/spitting-dilophosaurus) put counters on enemy creatures early, giving the Reaper something to steal.

## Key cards

- [Necroskitter](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/48/necroskitter) steals like the Reaper, with no once-a-turn limit. With it out, [Black Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/71/black-suns-zenith) wipes the board and hands you every creature your opponents lose.
- [Village Pillagers](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/14/village-pillagers) has wither, so when it enters, every opposing creature gets a -1/-1 counter.
- [Maha, Its Feathers Night](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/100/maha-its-feathers-night) sets every opposing creature's base toughness to 1, so a single counter kills most of them.
- [Nest of Scarabs](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/101/nest-of-scarabs), [Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/123/hapatra-vizier-of-poisons) and [Flourishing Defenses](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/107/flourishing-defenses) turn your counters into 1/1 tokens. [Obelisk Spider](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/141/obelisk-spider) drains each opponent for 1 whenever you place counters.

## How it wins

The everyday win is a slow-motion heist: the Reaper takes a creature a turn, tokens pile up, and Obelisk Spider and [Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/317/sephiroth-fabled-soldier-sephiroth-one-winged-angel) drain the table. Spitting Dilophosaurus keeps enemy creatures with counters from blocking.

The fast kill is a chain reaction. Get [Blowfly Infestation](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/46/blowfly-infestation) out alongside one of the token makers above, then put a counter on a 1/1 token. It dies, so Blowfly puts a counter on another 1/1, and that counter makes a fresh token to go next. Add Sephiroth, Obelisk Spider or [All Will Be One](https://scryfall.com/card/pza/8/all-will-be-one), and the laps drain everyone else to zero. To stop, put the counter on something bigger. It usually comes late, and tutors like [Demonic Tutor](https://scryfall.com/card/dvd/49/demonic-tutor) help find the pieces.

## What beats it

- Graveyard hate that exiles dying creatures: nothing dies, so nothing comes back.
- Blowfly Infestation, Nest of Scarabs and Flourishing Defenses fall to anything that hits enchantments.
- Decks with few creatures leave the Reaper nothing to take, and +1/+1 counters cancel -1/-1 counters one for one.
- Repeated removal on the Reaper. [Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/50/deflecting-swat) costs nothing while it's out, and [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/34/heroic-intervention) protects your whole board for a turn.
