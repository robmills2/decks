Themes: Gaining life, Draining opponents, Vampires
Difficulty: Medium

## What it wants to do

It's death by a thousand cuts. The deck slowly drains life from the table, and Vito (and some of his friends) turn lifegain into damage. With the right combo in place, one small gain becomes a loop that ends the game. Or drop a bomb and kill a player in a single turn. 

## The commander

[Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2282/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose) is low-cost, but fragile. Each time you gain life, he makes an opponent of your choice lose that much, so you can spread the damage or focus one player. For five mana he gives your creatures lifelink for the turn, feeding his ping effect. Protect him with [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/fdc/291/swiftfoot-boots).

## Opening hand

- Keep three or four lands, or two lands and a cheap mana rock.
- Look for a cheap way to gain life, such as [Vampire of the Dire Moon](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/120/vampire-of-the-dire-moon), [Creeping Bloodsucker](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/415/creeping-bloodsucker) or [Pristine Talisman](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/151/pristine-talisman).
- Send back hands with one land or only expensive cards. Vito starts in the command zone, so you never need him in hand.

## Key cards

- [Exquisite Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/avr/102/exquisite-blood) and [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/58/bloodthirsty-conqueror): whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much. With Vito, that's the loop.
- [Sanguine Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1799/sanguine-bond) and [Enduring Tenacity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/400/enduring-tenacity) do Vito's job if he's removed.
- [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/89/gray-merchant-of-asphodel) drains each opponent for every black mana symbol on your permanents, and Vito sends the total you gain at one player.
- [Whip of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/110/whip-of-erebos) gives all your creatures lifelink.
- [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/336/bloodletter-of-aclazotz) doubles what opponents lose on your turn.

## How it wins

Usually slowly: your drains hit everyone each turn, Vito adds a second hit to each, and Exsanguinate or Torment of Hailfire finishes whoever is left.

The fast way: with Vito and Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror out, any life gain starts a loop. An opponent loses 1, you gain 1, and so on until every opponent is at 0. It runs until the game ends unless someone removes a piece. Both combo pieces cost five, so it rarely happens before turn five or six.

[Blood Tribute](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/81/blood-tribute) kills one player: tap Vito to kick it, and they lose half their life, you gain it, and Vito takes care of the rest.

## What beats it

- Anything that stops you gaining life shuts the deck down.
- Instant-speed removal can break the loop while it runs. [Imp's Mischief](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/167/imps-mischief) can redirect one removal spell.
- Enchantment removal hits Exquisite Blood, Sanguine Bond and Whip of Erebos.
- With only two board wipes, a wide army can get ahead of you. [No Mercy](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/427/no-mercy) makes attacking you costly.
- Once the table knows about the loop, expect Vito to die on sight.

## Sideboard

For a Bracket 2 table, take out Exquisite Blood, Bloodthirsty Conqueror and Blood Tribute, and put in [Pontiff of Blight](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/768/pontiff-of-blight), [Vein Ripper](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/110/vein-ripper) and [Grave Venerations](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/9/grave-venerations). That removes the loop and OTK.

- Pontiff gives all your creatures extort, so each spell you cast lets you pay extra black mana to drain everyone a little more.
- Vein Ripper drains an opponent for 2 whenever any creature dies.
- Grave Venerations makes you the monarch. While you keep it, you draw an extra card and get a creature card back from your graveyard at the end of each of your turns. It also drains each opponent for 1 whenever one of your creatures dies.
