Themes: Making everyone discard, Punishing discards, Stealing from graveyards
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

Nobody at the table gets to keep their cards, not even you. Everyone discards, turn after turn, and every card an opponent throws away costs them life. Tinybones picks through their graveyards and plays the best finds himself. Once their hands are empty, the other Tinybones takes 10 life from each of them.

## The commander

[Tinybones, the Pickpocket](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2773/tinybones-the-pickpocket) is cheap, deadly and very small: one mana for a 1/1 with deathtouch. Whenever he deals combat damage to a player, you may cast a creature, artifact, enchantment or planeswalker from that player's graveyard, paying with any kind of mana. You cast it right then, so keep mana open when he attacks. A blocker stops him cold, so [Brotherhood Regalia](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/71/brotherhood-regalia) and [Whispersilk Cloak](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/7182/whispersilk-cloak), which make him unblockable, are his best friends.

## Opening hand

- Keep three lands, or two lands and a cheap rock like [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/267/sol-ring) or [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/297/arcane-signet).
- Cheap discard like [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/20/thoughtseize), [Hymn to Tourach](https://scryfall.com/card/fem/38d/hymn-to-tourach) or [Burglar Rat](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/170/burglar-rat) starts filling graveyards for Tinybones to raid.
- An unblockable equipment is worth keeping a hand for. Tinybones costs one, so he can start attacking on turn two.

## Key cards

- [Bottomless Pit](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2775/bottomless-pit), [Necrogen Mists](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2776/necrogen-mists), [Cunning Lethemancer](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/69/cunning-lethemancer) and [Oppression](https://scryfall.com/card/wot/32/oppression) make every player discard again and again, you included. Play out your own hand first, then put them down.
- [Megrim](https://scryfall.com/card/8ed/143/megrim), [Liliana's Caress](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/M11-103/lilianas-caress), [Raiders' Wake](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/116/raiders-wake) and [Fell Specter](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/96/fell-specter) each take 2 life from an opponent for every card they discard. With one out, [Dark Deal](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/66/dark-deal) (everyone discards their hand and draws one fewer) costs each opponent 2 life per card they held.
- [Waste Not](https://scryfall.com/card/m15/122/waste-not) and [Geth's Grimoire](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/123/geths-grimoire) turn their discards into cards, mana and Zombies for you. [Bone Miser](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/15/bone-miser) does the same with yours.
- [Fireshrieker](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/674/fireshrieker) gives Tinybones double strike: two hits, two thefts.

## How it wins

Most games, the table bleeds 2 life at a time while Tinybones helps himself to their discards. When hands run dry, [Tinybones, Trinket Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2458/tinybones-trinket-thief) closes it out: for six mana, each opponent with no cards in hand loses 10, as often as you can pay. [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/360/cabal-coffers) and [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/368/crypt-ghast) make that mana, or pay for a big [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/276/exsanguinate) or [Torment of Hailfire](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/77/torment-of-hailfire). Torment is cruel here: a player with no cards can't discard to it, so each time they lose 3 life or sacrifice something that isn't a land.

## What beats it

- Enchantment removal: most of the discard engines and punishers are enchantments.
- Graveyard removal leaves Tinybones nothing to steal.
- Decks that want cards in the graveyard, like reanimator, thank you for the discard.
- Fast decks full of cheap cards empty their own hands before the discard matters.
- Your own life total: [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/20/thoughtseize), [Infernal Grasp](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/84/infernal-grasp) and [Black Market Connections](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/620/black-market-connections) all charge life.
- Tinybones is a 1/1, so everything kills him. [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/275/swiftfoot-boots) and [Lightning Greaves](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1493/lightning-greaves) protect him, and he's cheap to recast.
