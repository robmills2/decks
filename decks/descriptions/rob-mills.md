Themes: Milling, Untapping your commander, Casting from the graveyard
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

Ever wanted an anime mermaid to get your deck wet? Wish granted! Milling moves cards from the top of a library to the graveyard, and anyone who has to draw from an empty library loses. Neerdiv mills a little with every tap, until a loop taps and untaps it endlessly, emptying every opponent's library in one turn. Until then, the deck counters threats and digs for the pieces.

## The commander

[Neerdiv, Devious Diver](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/36/neerdiv-devious-diver) starts as a modest 2/2, but it never stops growing. Whenever it becomes tapped, a player you choose mills cards equal to its power. Whenever you cast a spell from your graveyard, or use an ability of a card there, you draw a card and Neerdiv gets a +1/+1 counter. Every flashback spell makes the next mill bigger.

## Opening hand

- Two or three lands and cheap spells will do: Neerdiv costs three, and most spells cost less.
- [Mystic Remora](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/406/mystic-remora) and [Rhystic Study](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/45/rhystic-study) draw you a card for opponents' spells unless they pay extra. Both are best early.
- A tutor or a loop piece is a bonus.
- Send back hands with one land or no card draw.

## Key cards

- [Freed from the Real](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/38/freed-from-the-real) and [Pemmin's Aura](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/SCG-45/pemmins-aura) untap Neerdiv for one blue mana. Freed can also tap it, so after [High Tide](https://scryfall.com/card/fem/18b/high-tide), each Island you tap is another mill.
- [Paradise Mantle](https://scryfall.com/card/mma/211/paradise-mantle) and [Relic of Legends](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/209/relic-of-legends) let Neerdiv tap for mana. Either one plus either aura is the loop.
- [Agatha's Soul Cauldron](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/242/agathas-soul-cauldron) gives Neerdiv the abilities of creature cards it exiles. Exile [Aphetto Alchemist](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/ONS-64/aphetto-alchemist), and Neerdiv can tap to untap itself, over and over: a second loop.
- [Quiet Speculation](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/68/quiet-speculation) puts three flashback spells from your library into your graveyard. [Snapcaster Mage](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/50/snapcaster-mage) and [Mission Briefing](https://scryfall.com/card/grn/44/mission-briefing) let you cast others from there.

## How it wins

With the loop together, tap Neerdiv for blue mana, and a player you choose mills. Spend that mana to untap it, and repeat as often as you like. Empty every opponent's library: they lose when they next draw, or right away if you make them. [Archmage's Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/8/archmages-charm) and [Deep Analysis](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/65/deep-analysis) each make a player draw two.

With [Thassa's Oracle](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/73/thassas-oracle) in hand, mill yourself instead, then cast it with your library empty: you win on the spot.

Without the loop, Neerdiv mills with each attack, and High Tide plus Freed takes a big bite out of a library.

## What beats it

- Removal on Neerdiv, worst of all while it wears an aura: the aura goes too. [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/258/swiftfoot-boots) and Pemmin's Aura stop removal that targets it, and [March of Swirling Mist](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/61/march-of-swirling-mist) can hide it until your next turn.
- Artifact and enchantment removal: the loop needs an aura and an artifact.
- Graveyard decks. Milling them hands them cards to bring back, so save [Tormod's Crypt](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/115/tormods-crypt) for them.
- Wide boards: the deck has few answers to creatures, and only [Cyclonic Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/35/cyclonic-rift) clears the table.
