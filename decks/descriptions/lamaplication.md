Themes: Casting cheap spells, Multiplying Monks, Combat buffs
Difficulty: Hard

## What it wants to do

One monk walks in. A whole monastery walks out. A quarter of the deck costs one mana, and each noncreature spell you cast makes Elsha bigger for the turn. Every time Elsha hits a player, it recruits a Monk for each point of damage, and your spells pump them too. With double strike and a few spells, Elsha can knock out a player in one combat.

## The commander

[Elsha, Threefold Master](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/2/elsha-threefold-master) is a scrawny 1/1 for three that bulks up fast. It has trample and prowess: each noncreature spell you cast gives it +1/+1 until end of turn. Whenever it deals combat damage to a player, you create that many 1/1 Monks, and they have prowess too. It's the whole engine, and everyone knows it.

## Opening hand

- Elsha needs blue, red and white on turn three. Every fetch land here can find any of the three, and [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/105/arcane-signet) makes any of them. White is the one you'll most often lack.
- Look for two or three one-mana spells that draw a card and help Elsha connect, like [Slip Through Space](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/47/slip-through-space) (unblockable) or [Expedite](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/108/expedite) (haste).

## Key cards

- [Monastery Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/125/monastery-mentor) makes a Monk for every noncreature spell, no combat needed.
- [Bria, Riptide Rogue](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/379/bria-riptide-rogue) gives your other creatures prowess, so each Monk gets two, and every noncreature spell makes one of your creatures unblockable.
- [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/180/jeskai-ascendancy) gives your whole team +1/+1 for every noncreature spell.
- [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2415/roaming-throne), naming Monk, makes Elsha's token ability and every Monk's prowess trigger twice. [Veyran, Voice of Duality](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/310/veyran-voice-of-duality) doubles whatever your instants and sorceries set off.
- [Boros Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/721/boros-charm) and [Psychotic Fury](https://scryfall.com/card/dis/68/psychotic-fury) give Elsha double strike: two hits, two batches of Monks.

## How it wins

Through the front door, mostly. Elsha connects, the Monks pile up, and every spell pumps the army.

The quick kill is commander damage: 21 combat damage from one commander knocks a player out. With double strike, Elsha needs just 11 power, and Jeskai Ascendancy or Roaming Throne makes each spell add 2.

[Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/14/purphoros-god-of-the-forge), [Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/card/wot/44/impact-tremors) and [Warleader's Call](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/242/warleaders-call) hit every opponent whenever a creature enters on your side. A 6/6 double-striking Elsha makes twelve Monks: 24 damage to each opponent from Purphoros alone.

For one huge turn, [Underworld Breach](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/161/underworld-breach), [Mizzix's Mastery](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/41/mizzixs-mastery) and [Will of the Jeskai](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/40/will-of-the-jeskai) replay your graveyard's spells, and [Vivi Ornitier](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/248/vivi-ornitier) pings every opponent for each.

## What beats it

- Removal on Elsha in response to your pump spells wastes the whole turn. [Fierce Guardianship](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/35/fierce-guardianship) and [Force of Will](https://scryfall.com/card/all/28/force-of-will) can counter it without spending mana.
- Board wipes sweep away the Monks. [Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/343/teferis-protection) and [Clever Concealment](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/125/clever-concealment) hide your board until your next turn, and Boros Charm can make it indestructible.
- Graveyard removal shuts off the big turn.
- Taxes on spells, or limits on how many you can cast, choke the deck.
