Bracket 3: Upgraded1 deck

Get Sucked

BVito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose
Colors
Mono-black
Themes
Gaining lifeDraining opponentsVampires
Bracket
3 (Upgraded)
Difficulty
Medium

What it wants to do

Every time you gain life, an opponent loses the same amount. The deck gains life in small, steady ways: lifelink creatures, a mana rock that gains 1 when it taps, and creatures that drain whenever something dies. Vito turns each gain into damage, so the table bleeds every turn. With the right enchantment or creature, one small gain becomes a loop that ends the game.

The commander

Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose costs three, so cast him on turn three. Each time you gain life, he makes an opponent of your choice lose that much, so you can spread the damage or focus one player. For five mana he gives your creatures lifelink for the turn, so each hit lands again on an opponent you pick. Protect him with Swiftfoot Boots.

Opening hand

  • Keep three or four lands, or two lands and a cheap mana rock.
  • Look for a cheap way to gain life, such as Vampire of the Dire Moon, Creeping Bloodsucker or Pristine Talisman.
  • Send back hands with one land or only expensive cards. Vito starts in the command zone, so you never need him in hand.

Key cards

  • Exquisite Blood and Bloodthirsty Conqueror: whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much. With Vito, that's the loop.
  • Sanguine Bond and Enduring Tenacity do Vito's job if he's removed.
  • Gray Merchant of Asphodel drains each opponent for every black mana symbol on your permanents, and Vito sends the total you gain at one player.
  • Whip of Erebos gives all your creatures lifelink.
  • Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubles what opponents lose on your turn.

How it wins

Usually slowly: your drains hit everyone each turn, Vito adds a second hit to each, and Exsanguinate or Torment of Hailfire finishes whoever is left.

The fast way: with Vito and Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror out, any life gain starts a loop. An opponent loses 1, you gain 1, and so on until every opponent is at 0. It runs until the game ends unless someone removes a piece. Both pieces cost five, so it rarely happens before turn five or six.

Blood Tribute kills one player: tap Vito to kick it, and they lose half their life, you gain it, and Vito takes the rest.

What beats it

  • Anything that stops you gaining life shuts the deck down.
  • Instant-speed removal can break the loop while it runs. Imp's Mischief can redirect one removal spell.
  • Enchantment removal hits Exquisite Blood, Sanguine Bond and Whip of Erebos.
  • With only two board wipes, a wide army can get ahead of you. No Mercy makes attacking you costly.
  • Once the table knows about the loop, expect Vito to die first.

Sideboard

For a Bracket 2 table, take out Exquisite Blood, Bloodthirsty Conqueror and Blood Tribute, and put in Pontiff of Blight, Vein Ripper and Grave Venerations. That removes the loop and the Blood Tribute kill.

  • Pontiff gives all your creatures extort, so each spell you cast lets you pay extra black mana to drain everyone a little more.
  • Vein Ripper drains an opponent for 2 whenever any creature dies.
  • Grave Venerations makes you the monarch. While you keep it, you draw an extra card and get a creature card back from your graveyard at the end of each of your turns. It also drains each opponent for 1 whenever one of your creatures dies.
